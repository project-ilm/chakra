Which siddhānta. CHAKRA computes true (dṛk) positions from the ephemeris. That places it with the Bishuddha / Dṛk Siddhānta school. The Gupta Press and other odṛk panjikas follow Sūrya Siddhānta parameters and will differ on some festival dates — this is a real, long-standing divergence between the two schools, not an error in either. Where your community follows Gupta Press, use its dates. Nothing here is a ruling.

What a panjika carries

Present here

  • Bengali year and month, from the solar saṅkrānti
  • Daily vāra, tithi, nakṣatra, yoga, karaṇa
  • Sunrise, sunset, day length
  • Festivals and fasts, each with the rule that produced it
  • Ceremony windows (bibāha, annaprāśana, gṛha-praveśa)
  • Eclipses in the year

Not here — and why

  • Rulings on disputed dates. Two schools differ; we show ours and name the other.
  • Predictions. This is a calendar, not a forecast.
  • Directory matter — pilgrim listings, dialling codes, tide tables. Printed panjikas carry these; they are not astronomy.
